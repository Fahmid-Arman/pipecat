- `PerceptionReader` can read how the user sounded with an audio classifier, such as `HumeAudioDecisionsClassifier`. It keeps the audio of the user's turn, from a second before their speech, and sends the turn so far each time they pause. The final transcription gets the answer, waiting up to `timeout` seconds. A turn shorter than the classifier's `min_audio_secs` gets no reading. See `examples/expressive-conversation/expressive-conversation-hume-audio-decisions.py`.
